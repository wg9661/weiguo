About个人简介

Hi, I am Wei, a PhD candidate in Photonics at EPFL, working in the Laboratory of Nanoscale Biology (LBEN) with Prof. Aleksandra Radenovic.

My research focuses on multidimensional measurement at the micro- and nanoscale, where I develop polarization-based super-resolution microscopy and low-noise solid-state nanopore sensors for fast in situ detection and precision metrology.

I earned my master's degree in Biomedical Engineering from Tsinghua University, advised by Assoc. Prof. Nan Zeng (Prof. Hui Ma's group), and my bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering and Automation from Yanshan University.

Research研究方向

I combine optical instrumentation, nanofabrication and reconstruction algorithms to measure molecules, cells and nanodevices in real time and under their native conditions: in situ, in cellulo, in vivo and in operando. 我将光学系统开发、微纳加工与图像重建算法相结合，在真实工作环境下实时测量分子、细胞与纳米器件的状态，包括原位（in situ）、细胞内（in cellulo）、活体（in vivo）以及器件运行中（in operando）的测量。

1. Polarization super-resolution and single-molecule imaging1. 偏振超分辨与单分子成像

Speed is the bottleneck of live-cell super-resolution. I developed polarization decoupling of fluorescent molecules: by capturing the polarization signatures of fluorophores across parallel channels, SPIFFI reaches super-resolution from a single exposure, bringing acquisition from minutes down to tens of milliseconds while also reading out dipole orientation. The same physics lets me follow the orientation and conformation of single molecules at solid–liquid interfaces. This work contributed to the lab's ERC Proof of Concept grant.

针对活细胞超分辨成像的速度瓶颈，开发了荧光分子的极化解耦技术：通过多通道并行捕获荧光分子的偏振特征，SPIFFI 仅需单帧即可实现超分辨，将成像时间从数分钟缩短至数十毫秒，并可同时读取偶极取向。基于同样的物理原理，实现了固液界面上单分子取向与构象的实时追踪。该成果为实验室获得欧盟 ERC-PoC 资助作出了贡献。

2. Solid-state nanopore sensing2. 固态纳米孔传感

We developed wafer-scale fabrication of ultra-low-noise solid-state nanopores with 5 nm features, together with a deep-learning signal reconstruction algorithm. Our codesigned platform measures DNA nanostructures at 10 nm resolution, a step toward next-generation molecular sequencing. [bioRxiv]

我们研发了具有 5 纳米特征尺寸的超低噪声固态纳米孔的晶圆级制造技术，并开发了配套的深度学习信号重建算法，实现了 DNA 纳米结构的 10 nm 分辨率测量，有望用于生物分子的下一代测序技术。[bioRxiv]

3. Polarimetric sensing and metrology3. 偏振传感与精密测量

Before EPFL, I built polarization scattering and Mueller matrix imaging instruments. A multi-angle polarization scattering system with an inversion algorithm sizes and identifies airborne particles down to the 500 nm scale in real time, a dual-wavelength Mueller matrix system unwraps polarization phase to remove the accuracy limit set by phase jumps in biological samples, and flat-field illumination with microlens arrays improves the image quality of Mueller matrix imaging.

在清华大学期间，搭建了偏振散射与 Mueller 矩阵成像系统：开发多角度偏振散射测量系统及属性反演算法，实现 500 纳米级颗粒的实时粒径检测与成分解析；提出双波长 Mueller 矩阵成像与偏振相位解包裹策略，解决了生物样品中相位跳变对测量精度的限制；利用微透镜阵列实现平场照明，提升了 Mueller 矩阵成像的图像质量。
